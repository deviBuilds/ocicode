# Panes

On web and desktop, the area beside the chat can show several panes at once: Browser, Terminal,
Files, and Diff each get their own pane, with their own tabs. Pull requests, devices, and side
chats share one more.

Use the buttons at the end of the chat toolbar to show or hide a pane. **More panels** holds the
rest and **Show right panel**, which brings back every pane you had open. The buttons stay in the
chat toolbar while panes are open; **Maximize panel**, in the panes' top-right corner, hides the
chat and carries the buttons until you restore it.

## Arrange panes

A new pane opens under the others. To put panes side by side, open the **Pane options** menu at
the end of a pane's tabs and choose **Move right** or **Move left**. Moving a pane past the last
column gives it a column of its own. **Move up** and **Move down** reorder a column, and **Hide
pane** closes the pane without closing its tabs.

Drag the line between two panes or two columns to resize them. Double-click it to even them out.
The layout is remembered for each thread.

## Open tabs

The **+** beside a pane's tabs opens another tab of that pane's kind: a new browser tab in the
Browser pane, a new terminal in the Terminal pane. Hold **+**, or right-click it, to choose any
kind of surface.

In a narrow window the panes fold into one set of tabs that opens over the chat.

The terminal drawer under the chat is separate from the Terminal pane. Its shortcut still opens
it; see [Keybindings](./keybindings.md).
